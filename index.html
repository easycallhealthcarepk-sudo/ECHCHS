About Us

Professional home healthcare, close to home

Easy Call Health Care Home Service was started to make quality patient care accessible without a hospital visit. We provide home nursing, clinical assistance, vitals monitoring, wound and post-operative care, and home sample collection for laboratory testing — delivered with the same care standard you'd expect in a clinic.

Our focus stays on convenient, dependable care at the patient's own home, handled by experienced staff who treat every visit professionally.

Clinical Assistant (CA) Certificate — PVTCRecognised technical training in clinical assistance and patient care procedures.
Pharmacy Diploma (B Category)Working knowledge of medicines, dosage handling and safe administration.
Experienced home healthcare providerHands-on experience across nursing, procedures and sample collection visits.
Founder & Owner — M. IsmailBased in Bahawalpur, overseeing service quality and every home visit.
